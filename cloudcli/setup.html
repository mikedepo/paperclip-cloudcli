What next

Tell Claude or Codex in CloudCLI what should be done and when, in plain words. It writes the job's instructions into your repo, schedules it in Paperclip and runs it once to check. Paperclip then runs it on time; every run's status, transcript and usage are on its dashboard, with the same login.

Try one of these in a new CloudCLI session (click to copy), or just say hi to hear what it can do:

Every weekday at 8:00, list the open issues in my repo with a one-line summary each.

Every Monday at 9:00, summarize last week's commits in my repo.

Jobs run once Claude or Codex is connected (step 1).
