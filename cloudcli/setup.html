Codex chats in CloudCLI off

Off unless you turn them on: Codex chats here run without permission prompts. Codex's sandbox can't start on Railway, so its chats work only in CloudCLI's Bypass Permissions mode, where Codex runs commands and edits files without asking you first. It is told to ask in the chat before anything destructive, but nothing enforces that. Claude chats still ask.

Codex chats need ChatGPT Plus or higher: on a free plan ChatGPT refuses the models CloudCLI offers, while jobs work. They take their own one-time code, since one ChatGPT sign-in can't serve both Paperclip and CloudCLI.

Your password

It was needed only at the first start: both apps now keep just a hash of it. You can delete ADMIN_PASSWORD from the Paperclip service's Variables in Railway, or seal it there (its ⋮ menu, Seal).

To change your password later, set ADMIN_PASSWORD there again, then redeploy Paperclip, then CloudCLI.

What next

Tell Claude or Codex in CloudCLI what should be done and when, in plain words. It writes the job's instructions into your repo, schedules it in Paperclip and runs it once to check. Paperclip then runs it on time; every run's status, transcript and usage are on its dashboard, with the same login.

Try one of these in a new CloudCLI session (click to copy), or just say hi to hear what it can do:

Every weekday at 8:00, list the open issues in my repo with a one-line summary each.

Every Monday at 9:00, summarize last week's commits in my repo.

Jobs run once Claude or Codex is connected (step 1).

Codex runs your jobs, but CloudCLI's chat has no assistant to ask for them yet: connect Claude, or turn on Codex chats (step 1). An assistant on your own computer can also create them (Optional: from your computer).
