# Contributing

Contributions are welcome: bug reports, questions and ideas as issues, fixes as pull requests.

- **Issues:** say what you did, what you expected and what happened. The deploy logs of the Paperclip and CloudCLI services help; remove your email, tokens and domains first.
- **Pull requests:** keep them small and say how you tested the change, ideally on a deploy of the template on Railway. Shell scripts must keep LF line endings (`.gitattributes` does this for you).
- Every merged change reaches existing deployments as an update their owners choose to apply, so we test each one on a fresh deploy and on an updated one before merging, and add a line to the [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md).
